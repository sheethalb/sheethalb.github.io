Experience and education

Before my current research at the Pattern Recognition Lab, I worked on medical imaging AI in industry, and before that spent a decade building imaging systems and software. That background in image pipelines, measurement and system-level benchmarking still shapes how I evaluate models today.

Research Scientist

2023–2026
Siemens Healthineers, Erlangen, Germany · alongside my doctorate at FAU Erlangen-Nürnberg
  • Chest X-ray and mammography AI: vision-language models for zero-shot classification, example-based lesion detection with few annotations, and training methods that raise sensitivity at clinically required specificity.
  • Patent application: Image-text deep neural network algorithm for patch-wise prediction of pathology findings (US App. 18/985,120, 2025).

Imaging Systems Engineer and Camera System Architect

2008–2014
Intel Corporation, Portland, Oregon, USA

Camera system architecture, power and performance

  • Defined the system-level power and performance indicators for mobile imaging use cases that served as the standard benchmark across development teams, and tracked them through hardware, firmware, Android and Windows software integration, validation and end-of-line test.
  • Built power, performance and bandwidth models, set targets for multiple products, and analysed how imaging workloads affect thermal design and hardware architecture (memory, storage, bus).
  • Wrote the system architecture specification for media data pipelines and translated customer requirements into platform targets, including projections for customer requests for information.

Systems imaging and image quality

  • Established the test methodology for power and performance measurement used by integration and validation teams, and profiled and optimised imaging pipelines across sensor, signal processor, encoder, graphics and CPU.
  • Helped set up an image-quality evaluation lab; defined key quality indicators adopted across Intel's validation teams and produced weekly competitive benchmarks of smartphone cameras.
  • Prototyped computational-photography and adaptive-filtering software on accelerator hardware.
